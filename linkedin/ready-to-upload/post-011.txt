The hardest lesson for every new supervisor in hospitality:

the shift should run well because of you — not through you.

If every towel, table and till needs your sign-off, that isn't high standards.

It's a bottleneck wearing a manager's badge.

And your best people feel it first. They stop deciding. Then they stop caring. Then they leave.

I put together a short self-test to check which one you're becoming — link in the first comment.

Which habit is hardest to let go of: checking, correcting, or approving?
